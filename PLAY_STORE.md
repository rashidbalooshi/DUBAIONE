# DubaiOne Play Store checklist

## App identity
- App name: DubaiOne
- Category: Business
- Primary contact: WhatsApp +971 50 176 8878
- Suggested package/application ID: com.dubaione.app

## Before release
- Generate the Android platform files with Flutter.
- Set the application ID to `com.dubaione.app` if available.
- Add final app icon and feature graphic.
- Configure release signing and keep the keystore private.
- Run `flutter analyze` and `flutter test`.
- Build the signed Android App Bundle with `flutter build appbundle --release`.
- Test the release build on a real Android phone.

## Store listing draft
Short description: UAE visa, PRO, business setup and document assistance in one simple app.

Full description: DubaiOne helps customers connect for independent assistance with common UAE visa, immigration, family sponsorship, Golden Visa, sponsor file, company formation, document clearance and government transaction procedures. Choose a service and contact DubaiOne directly through WhatsApp for assistance.

## Compliance
Do not present DubaiOne as an official UAE government app. Clearly disclose that approvals, eligibility and official fees are determined by the relevant government authority. Complete Google Play Data safety and privacy disclosures based on the final features included in the published build.
