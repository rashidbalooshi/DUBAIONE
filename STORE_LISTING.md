## App name
DubaiOne

## Short description
UAE visa, PRO, business setup and document assistance in one simple app.

## Full description
DubaiOne gives customers a simple way to explore independent assistance for common UAE administrative services and contact DubaiOne directly.

Available service categories include UAE visa services, Golden Visa assistance, family sponsorship, immigration procedures, sponsor file assistance, company formation, document clearance and government transactions.

Select the service you need, review the types of assistance available, then send a pre-filled enquiry through WhatsApp.

DubaiOne is an independent service assistance app and is not an official UAE government application. Government approvals, eligibility, processing times and official fees are determined by the relevant authorities.
