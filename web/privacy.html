DubaiOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

The current DubaiOne app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information inside the app.

If you choose to contact DubaiOne through WhatsApp, you leave the app and the communication is handled through WhatsApp. Information you voluntarily send may be used to respond to your service enquiry.

DubaiOne is an independent assistance service and is not a UAE government authority. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

This policy will be updated if future versions introduce analytics, forms, payments, accounts, advertising, cloud storage or other data-processing features.
