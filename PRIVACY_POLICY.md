# DubaiOne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

DubaiOne provides information and a direct contact path for independent UAE PRO and document assistance services.

The current app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information inside the app. When a user chooses to contact DubaiOne through WhatsApp, the interaction leaves the app and is handled through WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy practices. Information a user voluntarily sends in that conversation may be used to respond to the requested service enquiry.

DubaiOne does not claim to be a UAE government authority. Users should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information and should verify document requirements before sharing personal documents.

This policy should be reviewed and updated before publication whenever analytics, forms, accounts, payments, cloud storage, advertising, or other data-processing features are added.
